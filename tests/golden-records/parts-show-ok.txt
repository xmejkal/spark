Kingbright L-7113ID 5 mm red LED — indicator

  asks the host for:
    STATUS_LED   -> module pin A

  known:
    forward_voltage_v            1.9        verified
    forward_voltage_max_v        2.3        verified
    max_continuous_current_ma    30         verified
    peak_wavelength_nm           627        verified
    viewing_angle_deg            30         verified
  leg A: the anode, the longer leg
  leg K: the cathode, the shorter leg, by the flat of the rim

  the board must: A series resistor between the GPIO and the anode, always: without it the LED's current is set only by the pin's drive strength, and it either runs far past 30 mA or droops the pin's level.

  the board must: Keep it off the chip's strapping pins: an LED and resistor to ground load the pin while the chip samples its straps at reset, and can change how it boots. (Reasoned from the circuit, not from this datasheet.)

  the board must: On a breadboard: the LONG lead (anode) goes to the resistor; the short lead, on the side of the flat in the rim, goes to ground. Once the leads are trimmed equal, the flat is the mark that survives.
