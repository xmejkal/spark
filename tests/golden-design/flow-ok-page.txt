<section id="wiring">
<h2>Wiring</h2>
<p>Read the lines before power first.</p>
<h3>Before you power it</h3>
<ul>
<li>TactileButton A has no resistor: the firmware must pull it up with Pin.PULL_UP, as <code>pins.py</code> says (P204)</li>
<li>6x6 mm tactile push button: Decide the polarity against deep sleep, not convenience. MicroPython&#x27;s esp32.wake_on_ext1(pins, level) takes ONE level for every pin in the mask, on every chip (ports/esp32/modesp32.c, v1.29.0), so a board with two wake sources must have them assert in the same direction. Both directions are an OR on the S3 and C6: WAKEUP_ALL_LOW is a true AND only on the original ESP32, and ESP-IDF aliases it to ANY_LOW on every later chip (components/esp_hw_support/include/esp_sleep.h, v5.5.2). Prefer wiring a wake button to 3V3 behind a pull-down and arming WAKEUP_ANY_HIGH: low-level wake has an open MicroPython bug, micropython#17334 (&#x27;stuck pin&#x27;, reported on a C6, v1.25.0). Corrected 2026-10-01 (spark P63): this line used to say ALL_LOW is an AND here, which is false on these chips.</li>
<li>6x6 mm tactile push button: Debounce in firmware, not hardware, unless the button is also a wake source — a wake pin&#x27;s first edge is the one that matters and it is not debounced by anything.</li>
<li>Kingbright L-7113ID 5 mm red LED: A series resistor between the GPIO and the anode, always: without it the LED&#x27;s current is set only by the pin&#x27;s drive strength, and it either runs far past 30 mA or droops the pin&#x27;s level.</li>
<li>Kingbright L-7113ID 5 mm red LED: Keep it off the chip&#x27;s strapping pins: an LED and resistor to ground load the pin while the chip samples its straps at reset, and can change how it boots. (Reasoned from the circuit, not from this datasheet.)</li>
<li>Kingbright L-7113ID 5 mm red LED: On a breadboard: the LONG lead (anode) goes to the resistor; the short lead, on the side of the flat in the rim, goes to ground. Once the leads are trimmed equal, the flat is the mark that survives.</li>
</ul>
<p>Each record whole, with what it asks of the board: <code>parts.py --show led-red-5mm</code>, <code>parts.py --show tactile-button</code>.</p>
