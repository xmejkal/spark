# P282 and P247 bench: the board doctor and the live try on the XIAO ESP32-C6, 2026-10-17

The bench for P247 (#267, the live try) and P282 (#326, the board doctor). The PO worked the board and his terminal.
Claude ran the tries from `~/Development/blink-c6` on `/dev/cu.usbmodem1101`. The board ran the button-and-LED firmware
from P215, which keeps the board's own LED dark, so the LED shows nothing. Each result rests on one try (n = 1).

## Short version

- P247 passed on the real board: `print(1)` ran and the board was reset, `Pin(12)` was refused, and a try with no
  grant was refused. Nothing reached the board in a refusal.
- The reset is proven by the uptime: `time.ticks_ms()` said 104 s at 10:34:06, so the chip restarted at 10:32:22, one
  second after try 1.
- P282's doctor named the facts that Claude found by hand earlier. But a replug, not its "hold BOOT" step, made the
  board answer.

## What it means

- P247's value line holds on the board, except one part that this bench did not reach: the two causes after a cure
  that does not help. The fake-run tests cover that part.
- The doctor's "hold BOOT" step rests on one earlier try (p238 finding.md:52). This time a replug helped, and the PO
  did it before he read the step. So the bench neither backs nor refutes "hold BOOT". A replug is in `IF_NOT_HELPED`
  only after "press R".
- The bare `grant.py` failed, because nothing puts spark's scripts on the PATH. That is a candidate card.
