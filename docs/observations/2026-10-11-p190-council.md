generated printed nothing; the generated diagram now wires the board's serial port to the monitor
([#142](https://github.com/xmejkal/spark/issues/142)). About 40 hand-written lines and a regenerated bundle, so the
light council: one reviewer on Sonnet asking all six lenses' questions, then the refuter on Opus. Each finding is a row
in [`INDEX.md`](INDEX.md) (SR1–SR10).
