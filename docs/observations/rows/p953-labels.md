## The tests this PR changes

Each moved because a pad's label now says the steps' word and reads bottom to top, each part has a caption outside its
body, and the note has a line per part. Each keeps every other fact it pinned.

- 5 goldens `breadboard-ok-{c6,firebeetle,module,rebuild,xiao}.txt`. Before: pad labels as record keys (GND1, NC4) in white with a blue halo, reading top to bottom on a board; the part's id or short name in its body; one brown note line of joined sentences. Now: only their `breadboard.svg` lines move (read in `git diff`, and compared by script: every wire, pill and part picture byte for byte the same): the labels, the captions, the note's lines, and the canvas's height (c6 +0.8, firebeetle -4.4, module +12.6, rebuild +0.98). Their sheets, Wokwi files and records are unchanged. The guide's `button-light-xiao-breadboard.svg` follows the XIAO golden.
