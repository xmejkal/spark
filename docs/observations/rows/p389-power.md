# P389 PR 2: a board names its own charger, regulator, battery input and battery-level pin, findings rows

Findings from building PR 2 of P389 (2026-10-25, Prague). The build checked each claim of the approved plan in the code and in the kept sources.

| # | claim | source | status | evidence / where it went |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | GPIO14 on the T-Display is a battery pin | plan, round 1 | raised | it is not. The schematic calls the net on MTMS/IO14 `PWR_EN`, and the kept README lists it as `ADC Power 14`. The fixture's `//power` says so and holds no entry for it. What `PWR_EN` switches (Q3 and D6 are near it) was not read |
| 2 | Facts of an entry that is not offered still reach `pins.py` | council after PR 1 | raised | the gate in `onboard_lines` did not move, on purpose: PR 1 pinned that a display on pins with no names still writes its width and height. The drift goes to P405 (#529) |
| 3 | The `LABEL` widening the PO allowed has a caller | plan, round 1 | acted | it has none: the battery entry holds GPIO34 only, with no labels. `LABEL` stays `[A-Z0-9]+` (rule 6.1) |
| 4 | `--offer` shows an onboard part's facts | build | raised | it shows none. `Offer.from_record` keeps `power` as one text capped at 1000 characters, and the `power` blocks of the FireBeetle and the XIAO are longer, so their new facts are cut there. A card "the offer shows an onboard part's facts" is proposed for the next council |
| 5 | The offer words `onboard charger`, `onboard voltage regulator`, `onboard battery input` and `onboard battery level` serve a need | plan, round 2 | acted | `matching.same_words` accepts an offer when every need word is in it. Misses, pinned by a test: `power` / `battery`, `power` / `battery charger`, `sense` / `voltage`, `connect` / `charger`. A need `sense` / `battery` does match the battery level, and that is meant. `commands/idea.md` names the four need words that match |
| 6 | The divider on the T-Display is 2, from R6 and R7 at 100K 1% each | plan | acted | read in a 600 dpi render of the kept schematic: BAT to R6 to net BAT_ADC to R7 to GND, with C22 10nF across R7. Both labels read `100K%1`. BAT_ADC reaches pin 10 `VDET_1/IO34` of U3. The fact is verified. The value 2 assumes the ADC input draws no current |
| 7 | The T-Display has a battery connector with a known type | plan | raised | the schematic draws P2, two pins, marked 锂电池 (lithium battery), pin 1 on the battery net and pin 2 on GND. It names no connector type and no pitch. The fact says so |
| 8 | TP4054 and the regulator are named in the kept schematic | plan | acted | U6 TP4054 (title 锂电池充电电路) and U7 `AP2112K-3.3V(RT9013)`, IN +5V and OUT VDD3V3. The fixture copies the label as printed, not "AP2112K-3.3 (or RT9013)" |
| 9 | The FireBeetle has a battery input a kept source names | plan | raised | none. The V1.3 schematic draws J1 as a battery symbol between `VBAT+` and `BAT-` with test points TP9 and TP10, and no pads or connector. No `battery_input` on the FireBeetle. The wiki is not kept |
| 10 | The FireBeetle regulator is one named chip | plan | acted | two: AXP313A (V1.1 and earlier) and TPS62A02 (V1.2 and later). The V1.3 schematic names `TPS62A02DRLR` at U1. The V1.0 schematic shows a chip with a `DCDC1` output and no part name in its text. So the fact is unverified and says why it matters. The offer ignores `verified`, so the board is offered for `onboard voltage regulator` either way |
| 11 | The XIAO has a `battery_input` a kept source names | plan | acted | the kept wiki, Battery Usage, names two pads: negative near D8 and positive near D5. The fact quotes the sentence. It says "should be located", so it is the maker's instruction, and the kept schematic names no pad part for it. The schematic has test points TP9 and TP10 on VBAT |
| 12 | The shipped records cite as `documents.<id>, <place>` | build | acted | the facts beside them in the XIAO and the FireBeetle use prose plus `cites`. The new facts do the same. The T-Display fixture uses `documents.<id>, <place>` as its other facts do |
| 13 | Two tests expect fact lines from a `gpio: []` entry and need one GPIO | plan, round 1 | acted | round 2 dropped it, and the tests stay as they are (`test_a_fact_is_a_line_only_when_its_value_is_a_finite_number_or_a_word`) |
| 14 | `is_fact_value` refusing a blank word alters no line of a kept record | plan | acted | held: every library board validates, and no shipped fact holds a blank word. A test pins that a blank fact writes no `pins.py` line |
| 15 | A label and a fact of one entry with the same `pins.py` name are refused | plan | acted | held: `role_entry_problems` says "has the name of the label". `test_a_fact_may_not_take_the_name_of_a_label` |
| 16 | The battery role costs what the plan names | plan | raised | the plan gives no number. The build chose 20, the button's, because a divider is wired to the pin like a button is. The numbers are ordinal. A test pins that it costs at least the LED's and less than the console's |
| 17 | `tests/test_xiao_record.py` leaves the XIAO's `power` alone | build | acted | it compared the whole block with the bench-checked record. It now compares the facts that record holds and names the three new ones. The bench record is a demo project and is not changed |
| 18 | `test_self_confirmation` lets a test read the new constants | build | acted | `boards.ONBOARD_BATTERY_SENSE` has its entry in `MAY_READ`, as the other role names have. The default penalty is not read by a test |
| 19 | A `power` fact needs a pin role or a table row of its own | council after PR 1 | acted | neither: `POWER_FUNCTIONS` is a table of three functions, because a power fact has no pins, no constant and no scenario. `OnboardRole` stays for parts with pins |
| 20 | The mutation table holds a row for the new logic | plan | acted | nine rows added to `p353-onboard-roles` (21 in all): the blank word, the row check, the shared value check, the power check, the value gate of the offer, the battery words, the limit sentence, the penalty and the conflict list |
