# P251: the cure at the bench on the XIAO ESP32-C6, 2026-10-15

The bench for P251 (#264), by the protocol in [the story plan](../2026-10-15-p251-reach-plan.md). The PO replugged the
XIAO ESP32-C6 straight into the Mac's own port, and Claude ran `scripts/reach.py --project . --json` from
`~/Development/button-light` (branch `p251-reach`, commit 7913768) after each replug. The board ran the button-light
firmware. The JSON lines are kept in the session's scratchpad. The tables below hold every line.

## Short version

- PASS by D22's line. In 7 warm replugs, 3 boots were silent: mpremote said "could not enter raw repl". Each was cured
  by the open with DTR and RTS off and reached on the retry. The other 4 replugs reached the board at once.
- Each cure heard the ROM's `rst:0x15 (USB_UART_HPSYS)` line and the firmware's `ready`. So the open restarted the chip,
  and the rival "a plain delay cured it" (H3) does not explain these cures.
- Cold arm, 5 of 5 reached. Every cold open restarted the board: each heard the boot lines up to `ready`, with no
  mpremote call before it. The rival "the cure needs mpremote's DTR-True open first" is refuted by that. But the
  plan's own rule for the cold arm was `rst:` heard in 5 of 5, and only 3 of 5 heard it: in 2 runs the read began after
  the first boot lines, so the `restart_heard` column said false for a board that restarted (see the gap below).
- The cold arm also shows that the open restarts a board whose output was not dead. The cure is a restart, not a
  repair: a running program stops and starts again.

## Gaps and limits

- The restart detection misses a restart whose first lines came before the read began (cold replugs 1 and 3). The
  heard text began at `mode:DIO` and held `entry 0x4086b910` and `ready`. A fix is to count the ROM's `entry 0x..`
  line as a restart too. It is not in this PR: the warm arm, which D22 judges, heard `rst:` in every cure.
- Each first try ran 10 s or more after the replug, as the protocol said, but the time between the plug and the run
  was not measured. The times in the tables are when each line was written.
- The cure waits `PORT_SETTLE_S = 2` after the port is back. Every retry after it reached, so the guess held on
  this board, n = 3 cures.
- The cures in the reach test ran `resume exec` after the open (G7). reach.py runs a plain `exec`, which soft-resets
  first. It reached in every case here.
- One board, one firmware, one Mac. The silent-boot rate here was 3 of 7, near the reach test's 7 of 20.
