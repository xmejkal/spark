# P243 bench: deploy.py finds the XIAO C6 in download mode, and a press of R brings it back

Short version: the PO put the XIAO ESP32-C6 in download mode and ran deploy.py. deploy.py asked for R, saw the port go
and come back under the same name, and said `ready seen`. The 2026-10-14 first flash said `ready unknown` here.

- Date: 2026-10-14, 15:30. Board: Seeed XIAO ESP32-C6 on `/dev/cu.usbmodem101`, the Mac's own USB port.
- Project: `~/Development/blink-c6` (P236's board-only blink). Branch: `p243-download-mode` (PR #253).
- Steps: the PO held B and pressed R, ran `look.py`, then `deploy.py --token` in his own terminal, typed `yes`, and
  pressed R when deploy.py asked.

`DownloadModeProbe.wait_for_reset` sees it. Not tested: a board whose port comes back under another name, and Linux.
