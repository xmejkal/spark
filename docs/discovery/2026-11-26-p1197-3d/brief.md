## What we found <!-- CLAIMS -->
- [★C1] No record holds a 3D model. 30 of 43 records hold an outline in the board plane, and 3 of 43 hold a body's overall height above the board. Height facts sit under six keys, and `thickness_mm` means the bare board in two records and the overall height in two others · E pre-analysis.md section 1 (key scan of the repository and the store, shelf included) · caveat: 43 records on 2026-11-26, one store. A first count missed the shelf (council/architecture.md AR-3, council/po-assistant.md PA-3)
- [★C2] A module goes to the board with no body: `<chip footprint="pinrowN">` and no `cadModel` · E scripts/spark/design/board_sections.py:259 · caveat: none
- [★C3] tscircuit 0.0.2600, installed today, merges a local STEP body into its STEP export and its GLB. An STL body lands through the board in the STEP and breaks the GLB export · E pre-analysis.md section 4 (probe, 2026-11-26) · caveat: one run per variant on one probe board
- [★C4] tscircuit's STEP names a body it generates, or a JSCAD body, by reference (`PCB`, `U1`, `U2`) in one flat product. A STEP body that a part brings keeps the model's own solid names or none, so the reference is lost. kicad-cli's STEP is an assembly that names an instance by reference, but only for a part with a model · E pre-analysis.md section 4, council/tech-lead.md TL-2 and TL-3 · caveat: names in the file. Whether Fusion shows them is not run
- [★C5] tscircuit already draws a two-part printed case, `<enclosure.fdm.box>` (create-fdm-enclosure 0.0.4, MIT): a base and a lid with a lip and cut-outs per part. It has no mounting bosses yet · E the installed README, and the probe: two solids about 54 x 42 x 15 mm around a 50 x 36 mm board · caveat: version 0.0.4, the case was not printed or measured
- [★C6] tscircuit's 3MF export (`component-box-3mf`) fails in the installed CLI for a missing `manifold.wasm` · E probe log · caveat: one run, installed version 0.1.2113
- [★C7] Every library model found is CC-BY-SA, private-use or under unclear terms, except Adafruit's (MIT). So spark fetches a model at use time into the person's store and never commits it · E research.md section 1 · caveat: the terms of four sites were not read
- [★C8] No library found holds a model of the dev boards spark uses (DevKitC, C6 DevKitC, XIAO, FireBeetle). KiCad and Espressif hold modules (WROOM and others) only · E research.md section 1 · caveat: a vendor STEP of the FireBeetle 2 ESP32-S3 exists, because its record's note reads one (`//max_component_height`), but the record names no URL and no licence (council/tech-lead.md TL-12)
- [★C9] build123d (Apache-2.0) writes STEP with each shape's label as its name, and GLB, STL and 3MF, headless. Its kernel wheel is 62.5 MB · E research.md sections 1 and 2 · caveat: the install size on disk was not measured
- [★C10] Fusion's personal licence keeps STEP export, STL, scripts and add-ins, by Autodesk's blog of 2020-09-25. Its compare page of 2026-11-17 ticks no "File export" for personal use. The "80 cm²" limit is not on that page · E research.md section 3 (Wayback copies, because autodesk.com returned 403) · caveat: the two pages do not agree, and only a run on the PO's Fusion settles it
- [★C11] Fusion has a built-in MCP server that runs on the person's Mac while Fusion is open. Its page names no licence tier · E research.md section 3 (help.autodesk.com) · caveat: not turned on or run
- [★C12] Fusion 360, KiCad 10.0.6 and PrusaSlicer are installed on the PO's Mac. FreeCAD and OpenSCAD are not · E pre-analysis.md section 3 · caveat: installed is not used. How he uses them is an account question
- [★C13] TurboCase (MIT) makes an OpenSCAD case from a `.kicad_pcb`: posts at mounting holes, cut-outs from each part's box and a height property · E research.md section 4 · caveat: not run
- [★C14] A part's picture already comes from a chain of exchangeable makers (`PartPictureMaker`, each with `MakerUses` and a `Decline`), and the 2D drawing from a Strategy family (`ShapeSource`). The tools list maps a role to a tool. No 3D role and no 3D Operation exist · E scripts/spark/breadboard/picture_makers.py:1-60, scripts/spark/breadboard/part_shape.py:61-263, data/tools.json `roles`, scripts/spark/operation_catalogue.py · caveat: none
- [C15] kicad-cli writes GLB (109 KB on the probe) and a raytraced PNG · E pre-analysis.md section 4, P1089 finding section 2.1 · caveat: KiCad is optional for a person
- [C16] No perfboard layout or drawing exists, so a perfboard has no 3D start · E scripts/spark/flow/flow_codes.py:479, P1199 pre-analysis · caveat: none
- [★C18] The PO's own bin project already holds early pieces of this epic: module bodies through `cadModel` in `board.tsx`, a `board.glb` that its Makefile exports with `tsci export -f glb`, an offline one-page viewer (`tools/viewer-3d.js`, `tools/build-viewer.py`), and a check that reads each body from the GLB for overlaps (`tools/check-module-clearance.py`). It is MIT, by the PO, last commit 2026-10-12 · E ~/Development/smartbin-local (board.tsx:67-115, Makefile:198, tools/) · caveat: a sibling project, not spark code. Its viewer went stale once and was rebuilt to regenerate with each build (council/user.md US-1)
- [★C19] tscircuit's case leaves 6 mm above the board by default, and a part with no cut-out gives it no height. In the probe a module went 4.72 mm into the lid. The amplifier board (FireBeetle 2 S3, MAX98357A, a terminal) needs about 21.7 mm of depth with soldered modules or 27.7 mm in sockets, against the default 15.6 mm · E council/tech-lead.md PHY-1 and PHY-2 (calculated from record facts and common socket heights) · caveat: the socket and spacer heights are H. calculated, not printed
- [★C20] spark can write a module's STEP itself: about 110 lines of standard-library Python wrote an AP214 STEP of a box and a header row with 9 named solids, which occt-import-js, tscircuit and kicad-cli all read. Calling tscircuit's case solver directly took 5 ms, and its base and lid, added to a copy of the built `circuit.json`, exported as solids `Casebase` and `Caselid` with `board.tsx` untouched · E council/tech-lead.md TL-4 and TL-6 (scratch runs, deleted) · caveat: boxes only. Cylinders, holes and fillets need more code or build123d
- [★C21] A JSCAD body (`cadModel={{ jscad: plan }}`, a JSON plan that Python can write) reaches tscircuit's STEP and GLB named by reference, but tscircuit's KiCad export drops it, because only `stepUrl` or `wrlUrl` give a model line · E council/tech-lead.md TL-1 and TL-3 · caveat: a probe board, not a spark board
- [★C22] A missing model file fails silently: the build and the STEP export exit 0, and the part has no body. A model URL is fetched during the export, so that export reaches the network · E council/tech-lead.md TL-8 and TL-9 · caveat: one run each
- [★C23] No spark-built board exists yet to put in a case: no fab order is found, the walk P100 is parked since 2026-11-05, and the carrier board's mounting holes are "Not decided". The PO owns two bought DFRobot FIT0725 FireBeetle boxes · E council/po-assistant.md PA-13 and PA-14, council/user.md US-5 · caveat: H that no order exists anywhere outside the store
- [★C24] PrusaSlicer 2.6.1 on the PO's Mac is set for an Original Prusa MK3S with PETG, and PrusaSlicer imports STEP since 2.5. The five recent slicer projects are downloaded models, none a case of his own. Fusion 2705.1.15 is installed and has the built-in MCP setting. Its folders last changed 2026-10-07 to 10-09 · E council/user.md US-5, council/po-assistant.md PA-6 and PA-7 · caveat: installed and set up is not used. An update also changes folders
- [★C25] What the maker supplies on the spot is photos, and a photo gives no height. The e-paper outline came from a photo with about 5 % error. He asks spark for the holes and connectors ("do you have all the details in the record?", P934 diary 2026-11-20) · E council/user.md US-6, US-7 and US-10 · caveat: n = 1. Whether he owns callipers is not known
- [C26] The FireBeetle C6 shelf board holds 45 features in mm (USB-C, buttons, sockets, holes), none with a height. Mounting holes sit in five forms across the records · E council/user.md US-15 and US-16 · caveat: none
- [C27] `board_sections.emit` already takes 7 parameters against the limit of 5, and no `BoardEngine` base exists yet, so a body reaches the engine best as a resolved value through P1184's board writer · E council/architecture.md AR-8 and AR-9 · caveat: a proposal on the code's shape
- [C28] Eight open cards overlap the epic and are not listed on it. P735 #1010 writes the same hole and size facts · E council/po-assistant.md PA-16 · caveat: none
- [C17] One-image AI models give no metric scale, and photogrammetry handles thin shiny boards badly. A box with the photo as its top face gives the exact size from measurements · H research.md section 1 (the agent's judgement) · caveat: untested here
