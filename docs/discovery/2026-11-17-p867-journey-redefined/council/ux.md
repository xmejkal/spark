### E. Known patterns for a step that goes out of date (three sources read 2026-11-17)

- UX-23 · GOV.UK Design System, "Check answers": a summary page lists each answer with a Change link; a person who changes an answer sees the answer pre-filled and the page as it looked, then Continue returns to the summary; "They should not need to go through the rest of the transaction again"; if the change creates new questions, those come before the return to the summary. Its approach to an invalidated step: the summary is the single place that shows all answers, the change is local, and what the change makes necessary is asked next, not left for the person to find · E · https://design-system.service.gov.uk/patterns/check-answers/ , read 2026-11-17 (WebFetch) · supports only: what a public design system recommends for going back. It says nothing of a step already acted on in the world.
- UX-24 · Terraform CLI, `plan`: shows every action before any change, with symbols (`+` create, `-` destroy, `~` change in place, `-/+` replace) and a summary line ("Plan: 2 to add, 1 to change, 2 to destroy"); `plan` alone changes nothing; `-detailed-exitcode` returns 2 when the plan holds changes; it warns that state changes after a plan "might cause the final effect… to be different than what an earlier speculative plan indicated" and recommends to "re-check the final non-speculative plan before applying". The page I fetched does not say how a saved plan that went stale is refused (the answer did not cover it) · E · https://developer.hashicorp.com/terraform/cli/commands/plan , read 2026-11-17 (WebFetch) · supports only: the preview-before-change and the exit-code-for-changes pattern, as the build-tool answer; its page is silent on stale saved plans, so I do not cite it for that.
