# P261: standards for craft, quality and team process

Card: [P261 #283](https://github.com/xmejkal/spark/issues/283). Read on 2026-10-15.

## Short version

Spark already holds most of what the sources teach. It holds small steps, tests first, a retro, sizing to the change and
one writer for the repository. Spark is heavier than the sources in two places. Definition of Done item 9 repeats the council section, and W6 repeats counting
rules that the board gate holds. The sources ask for less. Spark is missing two light habits. One is
a short retro that runs more often than once per epic. The other is a rule for real-time talk: say "wait, I finish this
first", then come back. This finding proposes two adds, two merges and one cut. It adds no new cadence.

## The habit line to add (real-time talk)

Add under "Habits" in scrum/WORKING_AGREEMENTS.md, about three lines:

- When someone asks for something while you work, answer at once with one line. Say "wait, I finish this first, then I
  come back", and name the step you finish. Do not drop the work in hand, and do not go silent.
- Then come back at the step's end and say so. A real emergency (the PO says "expedite") interrupts at once.
- After three back-and-forth notes on one point, stop writing and talk it through live.

## Agent experiments

Two at most. Each one goes into the retro inbox with its reason, and the team judges it at the retro.

1. Weekly look with one retro line. Reason: the PO wants retros often and brief, and an epic can last weeks. The
   weekly look has no command and costs no agent. Judged at the retro by two questions. Did a change come out of the
   line? Did the line take under five minutes? If not, drop it.
2. Count whether small pull requests follow the sized table. Reason: the table already gives a light council under
   50 lines, but nobody counted yet whether small changes still get more. Judged at the retro by the count, and by
   the tokens per small pull request.

No new agent type is proposed. TEAM.md "The test each member has to pass" says an agent needs isolation, restricted
tools, fan-out or a context budget. None of the gaps meets that test, so scripts and the main thread cover them.
