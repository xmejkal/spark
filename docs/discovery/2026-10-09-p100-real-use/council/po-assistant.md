### Pre-mortem — six months on, P100 ran and told us nothing. Why? (H unless pointed; each is a proposed cause)

1. **The agent was measured, not the product.** The conversation (idea shaping, ways in, picks, firmware) is done by Claude under the PO's direction (VISION: "directs Claude rather than typing commands"); the gaps list then mixes spark faults with agent improvisation, and nobody can tell which. Guard: log per gap whether a documented command or a prose instruction failed (PO-5, PO-7). H.
2. **The predictions were written after the fact, or scored by their author.** The third cold test's scoring was the author's own (PO-5); the brief wants "someone who did not write them", with Q "who scores" still open. H.
3. **The pass line was soft.** "Basic scenarios fully covered" and "firmware that runs" with no named observable let a simulator pass, or a `check_spine` that exits 1 at a note (PO-1), count. D-less today (Q open in the brief).
4. **The round stalled on the one S3** (PO-3) and "runs on hardware" was replaced by "runs in Wokwi", repeating the third cold test's ending (PO-5, PO-6). E for the block; H for the replacement.
5. **The domain became research, not use** (PO-4): three researched records and a datasheet read per part turned the round into a test of `/spark:research`, which README already calls "done by agents, not run here".
6. **The store was mutated by the round** (picks, reservations, `used_in`, shelf entries, history lines) on the PO's real store, so P178's "as it is today" picture was changed by measuring it, and the next round starts on a different store. E for the mechanism (`--pick` writes `used_in`, seen in dry-run output); H that it will be done unwatched. Guard: scratch store per round, or a copy.
7. **Findings were not tied to a store version.** Fixes between P100 and P178 move the product; if gaps are not each filed with the command, version (0.8.1) and store state, the P178 council cannot tell a gap from a fixed one. H.
8. **n = 1 and the author's blind spot** (PO-9): all the findings are what the one person with the context sees; the memory file "background observers" already says the builder is the worst observer of their own blind spots. H, memory note.
