PH-4 · The FireBeetle S3's 3V3 budget, with the source's own provenance. Source `regulator_3v3_a` = 1.5 A, `verified: false`: the lower of two revisions, AXP313A DCDC1 1.5 A (V1.1 and earlier, X-Powers datasheet, not re-read) and TPS62A02 2 A (V1.2 and later, TI datasheet, not re-read). The module's own peak `module_peak_a` = 0.355 A, verified, ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 datasheet v1.1 Table 11 page 15 (802.11b 1 Mbps, 20.5 dBm TX, 3.3 V): datasheet-max. Headroom before any part = 1.5 - 0.355 = 1.145 A (calculated; 1.645 A if the board is V1.2+, which a person cannot be assumed to know). The record's own hardware-revision test is a photograph question ("look at the chip between the BOOT button and the USB-C"). · E (numbers) · boards/firebeetle2-esp32s3.json power.regulator_3v3_a, power.module_peak_a, hardware_revisions · supports: the page must show the headroom with "unverified" and the revision question beside it. Cheapest rung that settles the source rating: read the two datasheets (free, local, no bench); it cannot show what the real board's regulator delivers with the USB 5 V source weak (that needs a bench load test at the 3V3 pad with a meter, and a USB port or cable with a known limit).

PH-5 · The amplifier case, calculated (formula and inputs): peak output current into the speaker with a bridged output swinging V_DD = 3.3 V across R = 8 ohm: I = V/R = 3.3/8 = 0.4125 A; output power V^2/(2R) = 0.681 W (record: "a clipping estimate V^2/2R gives 0.68 W"). Supply current at output power P_out = 0.6 W (record, `verified: false`, read off a graph by eye) is P_out/(eta x 3.3 V): eta 1.0 gives 0.182 A, 0.9 gives 0.202 A, 0.8 gives 0.227 A. The efficiency eta is H: it is not in any record I read; it is an input the datasheet's efficiency curve would give. Sum with the module's peak: 0.355 + 0.202 = 0.557 A of 1.5 A at eta 0.9 (calculated, H on eta; not simulated, not measured). The record itself says its output on 3V3 may be too quiet and offers VCC as the alternative supply; p581's own row 9 says the assigner put the amplifier on 3V3 and lists the two unverified facts "listed, not decided". · E for the record text · parts/max98357a-dfr0954.json facts.output_power_w_8ohm_3v3 and why_it_matters; spark-walks/p581-sound-joystick/p581-rows.md row 9 · supports: the choice 3V3 or VCC for the amplifier is a safety-and-loudness decision a "pick" click must not make silently. The cheapest rung: a meter on the 3V3 pad during a loud passage on the bench; it cannot show the V1.1 versus V1.2 regulator difference.

PH-8 · A rail from off the board is shown with no voltage at all. ## Questions for the decider

Which facts must a step page show even with a sheet that is "ok", as a standing minimum: the rail headroom with its provenance (PH-4), the unverified figures under any ok (PH-16), the pin roles on the pins the design uses (PH-9)? · what it decides: the content list of the page's per-step panel, and which core descriptions (P481) must export power and role facts per step.
