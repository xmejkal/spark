## 3. Questions for the decider

1. For hand-written boards, may a project's rules file name a line, its two records and the reader pin, pointing at fact names rather than copying numbers, or must the bin gain a requirements file? Decides how outcome 2 (B25 on the bin) is reached.
2. May spark pass the fab's numbers to tscircuit's DRC (new sourced keys in `data/fabrication.json`), accepting that `/spark:build` may then fail boards that pass today? Decides the no-install DRC route against KiCad.
3. If simulation is wanted, which single route: native ngspice CLI (+1 tool, local, optional), tscircuit's CDN-loaded engine (+0, network at first run, remote code, circuit-JSON input only), or the npm engine (+1, offline, wrapper)? Decides the added-tool count and whether a run may need the network.
4. Is "switch to MCP" an agent-side registration only, with scripted checks always on the CLI (FE-18, 19), and is a contract or adapter change built with the first role that has two real backends, or before (W14)? Decides whether `scripts/tools.py` and `contracts` change now.
5. May corners be enumerated on assumed tolerances (rail ±3 %, carrier ±5 %), reported as `needs-measurement`, or must a source exist first? Decides how J1 and J3 stay honest under W1.
6. May one metered Wokwi scenario be spent on S5 and one on S7? Decides whether (5) and (7) get evidence before a design is chosen (W10).
