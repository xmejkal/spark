### SEN0526 (MEMS I2S mic, chip MSM261S4030H0R)

- PHYS-1 · Supply range: the datasheet disagrees with itself. Page 3 "Operation voltage 1.6 min, 3.6 max V"; page 7 pin table "8 VDD Power 1.8 to 3.3 V". The wiki says "Operating Voltage DC 3.3V" (stated). The record keeps 1.6 and 3.6 as verified and notes the conflict. Abs max "−0.3 to 4.0 V" (datasheet-max, p3). At 3.3 V, the margin to the pin-table upper edge is 0 V and to the abs max is 0.7 V (calculated 4.0 − 3.3). A 3.3 V rail with the usual ±5% is 3.465 V, past the pin table's 3.3 V by 0.165 V and inside 3.6 V. Which page governs is the open question. · E · datasheet (kept, `parts.py --kept SEN0526`) pdftotext p3 and p7; wiki per the diary; calculated · supports: supply tolerance claim; cheapest rung: the maker's current datasheet revision (no bench needed)
- PHYS-2 · Logic levels: inputs (L/R, WS, SCK) VIL max 0.25 × VDD, VIH min 0.7 × VDD (datasheet-max, p4). At 3.3 V: VIL 0.825 V, VIH 2.31 V (calculated). Host ESP32-C6 outputs: VOH min 0.8 × VDD = 2.64 V, VOL max 0.1 × VDD = 0.33 V (datasheet-max, ESP32-C6 datasheet v1.5 Table 5-4, kept p65). Margins: 2.64 − 2.31 = 0.33 V high, 0.825 − 0.33 = 0.495 V low (calculated). OK at 3.3 V with a common supply. The mic's output levels (VOH/VOL of SD) are not in the datasheet (no hits for VOH/VOL in a grep of the whole file); the host input threshold VIH 0.75 × VDD = 2.475 V cannot be checked against the mic's drive. — · E · pdftotext p4 of the SEN0526 datasheet; ESP32-C6 datasheet kept p65 (Table 5-4); arithmetic in code · supports: whether I2S levels need a check; cheapest rung: a scope on SD at 3.3 V is not needed for a 3.3 V host, only for a 1.8 V rail; the mic's own VOH needs the maker
- PHYS-3 · Pulls: schematic (read from the drawing by the walker, `verified: false`): R1 100 K from SD to GND; L/R (SEL) with no pull; CHIPEN to VCC.
