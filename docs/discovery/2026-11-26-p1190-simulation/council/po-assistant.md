### Pre-mortem: "It is six months after this shipped, and it failed. Why?"

Each judged against the MVP bar.

1. The step was built, and nobody ran it. The walks went from firmware to the bench as before (PO-1, PO-2). Against the bar: the screens did not move a project closer to working firmware.
2. The seam had one strategy. The second (the stand-in with sliders) grew with each firmware, as P295 warned, and was stopped half-done. Against the bar: the work went to tooling that did not serve it.
3. Claude wrote custom chips that passed firmware that then failed on the real part, like the ST7789 case the store's record warns of (PO-23). The person stopped trusting a "passed". Against the bar: a false pass moved the project backwards.
4. The free 50 minutes ran out, or Wokwi changed its plans, or the token step stopped the person. The paid run became the step that is always grey. Against the bar: neutral, but the step's promise failed.
5. The reference clock still showed grey after all of it, because the C6 has no open simulator and its one part skips (PO-16, PO-18). The PO's own example never showed the value. Against the bar: neutral.
6. Gate A was decided without the simulation (PO-4), so no later step read the simulation's result, and it went stale. Against the bar: neutral.
7. The epic's scope (five substeps, an editor, a result screen, three ways) delayed the v0.10 walk. Against the bar: negative.
