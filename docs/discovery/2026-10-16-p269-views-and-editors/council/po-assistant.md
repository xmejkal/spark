### Risks and assumptions

- PO-20 · Who is blocked now: no one. The PO is the only user (n = 1), and he can already see the breadboard in Wokwi by pasting and can lay out a perfboard by hand or in another tool. J2 and J3 say "nothing in spark" and "must paste". · E · jobs-and-journeys.md Journeys table rows 2, 3, 5 · supports only: "this is a want, not a blocker". The one friction with evidence is "changes are lost at the next build" (row 3, tagged H).
- PO-21 · The brief's Q0 says "no person outside the project used spark yet". The primary persona is a hypothesis. A large editor serves a persona nobody has met. · E · scrum/VISION.md:43 (quoted in jobs-and-journeys.md Who) · supports only: "value risk for a big editor".
- PO-22 · Beliefs that must be true, none tested (all H, from the request): (a) one stage-neutral model can feed breadboard, perfboard, schematic and PCB. (b) A hand-edited layout can survive regeneration. (c) Per-part pin positions in mm can be had for the parts in the drawer. (d) A web page in TypeScript can serve Artifact and Electron from one code base. (e) Pictures from the web can be kept without a licence problem. · H · the request in brief.md and jobs J1-J7 · supports only: "a list to rate in Q1".
- PO-23 · The riskiest belief by my judgement is (b) edit survival, together with (a). Reason: the PO's own words in this repo are "generated files must be diffable" and "the generator owns what is connected; you own what it looks like" (ARCHITECTURE.md). An editor that writes layout back creates a second writer on a generated file. #152 lists "two writers" as a failure of an earlier page. · H for the ranking, E for the two quotes · supports only: "a proposed riskiest, pending the decider (D)". The AT270 finding that the planner decided "jumper or leg" again is a small sign of the same drift (docs/observations/INDEX.md:1005).
- PO-24 · Cheapest test for (b), a proposal: take button-light's `breadboard.json`, move one part by hand in Wokwi, regenerate, and see whether the move survives and whether the diff stays readable. The merge code exists for `diagram.json` (PO-7). Cost: minutes, no new code. Pass line is the decider's to set (Q1). · H · from PO-7, PO-8 · supports only: "a test candidate, not a decision".
- PO-25 · Cheapest test for (c): count the parts in `parts/` (10 entries) and `boards/` (2) that have pin offsets in mm today. By PO-5 the answer from the one record I read is none. Then measure one through-hole part by hand and time it. · E for the counts (`ls parts`, `ls boards`), H for the plan · supports only: "the data cost per part is unknown; the PO measured Wokwi px by hand once and it took a session".
- PO-26 · Pre-mortem: "six months after it shipped it failed". Most likely stories, all H: (1) The editor was built on a Wokwi-px model, and the perfboard needed mm, so a rewrite followed (PO-2, PO-3, PO-4). (2) Edits lived in the editor's file, the build overwrote them, and the PO stopped trusting the page. (3) The catalogue editor made records with unverified numbers, which breaks "every fact cited" in the part records (PO-5 shows `verified` and `cites` on each fact). (4) Web pictures came with a share-alike or all-rights-reserved licence and had to be removed from a public MIT repo (PO-14, AGENTS.md). (5) It never reached the PO's hands because the stack choice (Vue, canvas, three.js, Electron) took the whole effort and the sheet never improved (VISION.md: "Judge a change by one bar … a breadboard with working firmware"). (6) The data work per part was larger than the view work and stalled at five parts (PO-3). · H · pre-mortem; supports only: "risk list for Q1".
- PO-27 · AGENTS.md says to judge a change by one bar, "does it move a basic project closer to a breadboard with working firmware?", and to "pause tooling that does not serve this bar". A perfboard stage, a catalogue editor and sharing do not obviously serve it. The breadboard view and an SVG in the sheet do. · E · AGENTS.md "Principles for changing spark", first bullet · supports only: "a tension for the decider; slice order should put the bar first".
