against 3).

   lines, #52, #53, #65, #101 and #114 (A1 §10 I1/I2). That is the PO's "start finishing more and not starting new epics

3. It builds what options 2–4 need — content digests per artefact, one envelope, one stage table. So A2's runner arrives
   as A1's I6 on state that already exists (A1 §10 I6), not as a rewrite of `check_spine`.
