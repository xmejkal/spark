# Risks and tests

Lens findings are cited by id from `council/`: EX (existing first), FE (feasibility), JO (the user's journey), RI
(risks), PH (physics). The electronics rows read as P136's question 3: for each category, what spark's checks cover
on the bin today and the cheapest rung that settles it (R0 desk · R1 formula · R3 digital simulation · R4 analogue
simulation · R5 breadboard · R6 bench), plus a board check (DRC or ERC on the layout), which the extension's rung list lacks. Every number carries its provenance word.

## Budgets <!-- REQUIRED:electronics-firmware:budgets -->
<!-- Energy per mode · peaks per rail against the source · dissipation per part, each number with its provenance and
the rung that would settle it (electronics-firmware:R<n>). -->
The bin's numbers, as the worked case a full check would have to compute; none is measured (nothing has touched
hardware).
- **Energy per mode:** on the LiPo and 3V3 side, awake 35.85 s per wake at 40–65 mA and 160 MHz, boot and the sound cue excluded, 0.40–0.65 mAh (calculated, PH-10); asleep 0.41–2.9 mA (calculated bounds, PH-5 to PH-8), so at an assumed 20 wakes a day the sleep floor is as large as the waking or larger; the ToF alone 0.36 mA with a target in view or 2.46 mA with none, at 2 Hz (calculated, PH-6). On the AA pack, the standing drain is 1.92 mA plus 3.4–4.5 mA for the module's LED (calculated, PH-8). Settled by R1, then R6.
- **Peaks per rail against the source:** 3V3 0.30–0.32 A average, 0.49–0.50 A at an 8 Ω speaker's peaks, 0.88–0.89 A at 4 Ω (calculated, PH-11, on an unverified 0.6 W amplifier at an assumed 0.9 efficiency), against 0.5 A in the rules file (stated, stale), 0.604 A of V33 copper (calculated) and a 1.5 A or 2 A regulator by revision (stated, unverified). The motor: 0.37–1.08 A at stall for 12–4 Ω (calculated, motor resistance assumed) against the L9110's 0.8 A continuous (datasheet) and the 1.5 A bound (stated). Settled by R1; the stall by R5.
- **Dissipation per part:** the L9110 0.7–2.0 W at stall for 12–4 Ω (calculated; no θJA anywhere); the motor's return trace a rise of about 79 °C at 1.5 A steady state (calculated, an IPC-2221 extrapolation; a stall of 1.5 s or less is not steady state). Settled by R1 for the bound, R6 by touch.
